Research

I work on efficient model adaptation: parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT), Mixture-of-Experts routing, adaptive vision-language models, and transferability estimation. I develop geometry-aware methods for selecting, routing and adapting pre-trained models without exhaustive fine-tuning. Representative papers are highlighted.

PEFTDiff: Diffusion-Guided Transferability Estimation for Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning
Prafful Kumar Khoba, Zijian Wang, Chetan Arora, Mahsa Baktashmotlagh
ICCV, 2025
paper

A diffusion-map metric that ranks PEFT methods for a new dataset without fine-tuning them all, cutting selection cost by 199× on VTAB-1k.

Feature Space Perturbation: A Panacea to Enhanced Transferability Estimation
Prafful Kumar Khoba, Zijian Wang, Chetan Arora, Mahsa Baktashmotlagh
WACV, 2025  (Oral Presentation)
paper

Spread-Attract perturbs the feature space to make transferability metrics more reliable, improving LogME by 28.8% across 11 datasets.

A Fine-Grained Vehicle Detection (FGVD) Dataset for Unconstrained Roads
Prafful Kumar Khoba, Chirag Parikh, Rohit Saluja, Ravi Kiran Sarvadevabhatla, C.V. Jawahar
ICVGIP, 2022
arXiv

The first fine-grained vehicle detection dataset for Indian roads: 5,502 images and 210k annotations across a 3-level type / make / model hierarchy.
